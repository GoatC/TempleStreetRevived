新莊妙妙廟 Temple Street Revived 是一個青年主導的計畫，用數位、低預算的方式，讓年輕人重新走進新莊廟口的廟宇文化。品牌的臉是一隻坐在廟頂上的有翼白貓：守著老街，也帶人走進老街。
Temple Street Revived is a youth-led project at Xinzhuang Temple Street (新莊廟口). It uses digital-first, low-budget ways to reconnect young people with temple culture. The brand's face is a winged white cat sitting on a temple roof, guarding the old street and leading people into it.

Everything here comes from the logo: its colours, its corners, its seal. Build from these tokens rather than from memory of the artwork.

## Voice 語氣

- Write for Gen Z and university students first, and for families with children second. Keep it warm, curious and a little playful, never preachy. 對象是大學生與年輕人，其次是親子；語氣親切、好奇、帶點俏皮，不說教。
- Lead with Traditional Chinese and follow with English. Put the English line in `wordmark-caps` (for a kicker) or `body` (for a sentence). 中文在前，英文在後。
- Treat temple culture with respect. Do not show or stylise deity figures. Draw on architecture, roof dragons, sacred objects, street life and the cat. 不以神像入圖；以建築、屋脊龍、器物、街景與妙妙貓為題材。
- Use the place names people actually use: 新莊廟口, 新莊廟街, 林協記文化館.

## Colour 色彩

- Ground every layout on `surface` (cat-white). Use `surface-tint` (feather-grey) for panels and bands, never as the page itself.
- Set text in `text` (ink-brown). `temple-red` is the one accent per view: a key button, a highlighted phrase, one solid block. Never use two red buttons side by side.
- Use `door-brown` for heavy solid fills such as footers, stamp plates and door-shaped frames, with `on-accent` (cat-white) text. Never pair it with ink-brown as a contrast (1.04:1).
- Keep `dragon-gold` for small precious things: seals, stamps, badges, a single underline. It is never a text colour on light grounds. On gold, set ink-brown text.
- Use `collar-grey` only through `hairline`, for 1px rules, dividers, control borders and quiet icons. Never use it for body text (4.4:1 on white).
- Proportion: about 70% cat-white or feather-grey, 20% ink-brown and door-brown, 10% temple-red, with dragon-gold as a touch.
- Light theme only. Do not place the logo or ink-brown text on dark grounds.

## Type 字體

- `mincho` (源流明體 GenRyuMin TW, regular and bold, included) sets everything people read: headings (`heading-1` to `heading-3`), `body` and `caption`. Its Latin glyphs carry the English as well.
- `calligraphy` (可畫鶴舞金書繁) is for one short display line per layout: `display-xl` or `display`, two to six characters, in temple-red or ink-brown. It exists only inside Canva (可畫), so make calligraphy headlines in Canva and export them. In code it falls back to GenRyuMin Bold.
- `wordmark-caps` echoes the arched TEMPLE_STREET_REVIVED wordmark: letter-spaced capitals for kickers and English sub-lines. Do not use it for sentences.
- Never fake the calligraphy with italics, outlines or other brush fonts.

## Shape and space 形與間距

- Blocks, bands and images are square (`radius-none`), like temple beams. Buttons, cards and inputs take `radius-door` (4px), the door block's softened corner. Seals, stamps and number badges are discs (`radius-seal`).
- Space in steps of `space-1` (8), `space-2` (16), `space-3` (24) and `space-4` (40). Separate sections with `space-4`.
- Use hairlines, not shadows. The brand has no shadow tokens: separate things with `hairline` rules or a `surface-tint` band.
- Motifs to borrow, as shapes rather than redrawn artwork: the upturned arch of the roof and wordmark, the round 莊 seal, and the tall door block (about 2:3).

## Logo 標誌

- Always use the supplied files in `Logos`. Never redraw, recolour, stretch, crop out the cat or re-set the wordmark in a font.
- Use `temple-street-revived-logo.png` (transparent, trimmed) on cat-white or feather-grey. Use `temple-street-revived-logo-on-white.png` where transparency is not supported, such as LINE rich menus, forms and print uploads.
- Clear space: leave at least the width of the door block (about 1/7 of the logo's width) on every side. At 280px wide, that is `space-4`.
- Minimum size: 160px wide on screen. Below that, the arched wordmark letters fall under 8px.
- Never place the logo on temple-red, door-brown, dark photos or busy patterns, because the ink-brown linework disappears.

## Iconography 圖示

- The brand has no icon set yet. Until it does, use simple single-weight line icons in `ink-brown` or `hairline`, and draw seals and stamps as `dragon-gold` discs with a single character in `mincho` bold.
- No emoji in brand materials.
